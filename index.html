Key finding: Churn in this dataset is largely rule-driven, not gradual. Any customer with a Monthly contract, Age ≥ 55, Support Calls ≥ 6, or Payment Delay ≥ 21 days churns at essentially 100%. Customers who trigger none of these thresholds churn at only ~17%. See the "Rule-Driven Thresholds" panel below.
